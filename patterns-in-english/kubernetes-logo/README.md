# Kubernetes logo

[Kubernetes logo](https://github.com/kubernetes/kubernetes/blob/master/logo/logo.png),
and its [usage guideline](https://github.com/kubernetes/kubernetes/blob/master/logo/usage_guidelines.md).

## Materials 
### Hook
2.00 mm

### Yarn
[Katia amigurumi cotton](https://www.katia.com/EN/yarns-spring-summer-unitedcotton-1279.html)

**B**=blue (1 is just enough, but better to prepare with more balls of yarn)  
**W**=white

## Pattern

- Make the color change always on the previous stitch unless instructed otherwise.
- Total stitch count of the round is in the parenthesis.
- The diameter of the final amigurumi is about 9 cm.

### Logo (make 2 pcs)

1. 7 sc MR B (7), close the ring and change color with sl by pulling W yarn through
2. 1 ch, inc x 7 W (14), sl
3. 1 ch, (1 sc, inc) x 7 W (21), sl
4. 1 ch, (inc W, 1 sc B, 1 sc W) x 7 (28), sl
5. 1 ch W, (1 sc W, 3 sc B in the same stitch, dec front loops only W) x 7 (35), sl
6. 1 ch B, (1 sc B, inc B, 1 sc B, 2 sc W) x 7 (42), end last stitch by pulling through B yarn, then sl with W yarn
7. 1 ch W, (4 sc, inc, 1 sc) x 7 (49), sl
8. 1 ch W, (3 sc, inc, 3 sc) x 7 (56), switch to B yarn at the last stitch, don't do sl but continue with circles instead
9. (4 sc B, inc W, 3 sc B) x 7 (63)
10. (3 sc B, inc B, dec W front loops only, inc B, 2 sc) x 7 (70)
11. (5 sc B, inc with 1 sc B and 1 sc W, 4 sc B) x 7 (77)
12. (6 sc, inc, 4 sc B) x 7 (84)
13. (7 sc, inc, 4 sc B) x 7 (91)
14. (8 sc, inc, 4 sc B) x 7 (98)
15. (9 sc, inc, 4 sc B) x 7 (105)

### Assembly

1. On the back side, sew in the yarn ends to avoid them from loosening or coming out over time.
2. Place the back sides together, ensure the end of the round of both pieces are aligned at the same edge.
3. Crochet B sc into each stitch, increase one sc at each corner (this would be round 16. i.e. increase at every 14th stitch).
   Start the round from the bottom of the pieces.
4. Fill firmly with fiber fill before finishing the round and after that finish it.
5. Sew in the yarn end.

> If you wish to add a hanger or a keychain ring, prepare for that before starting the assembly:

1. Crochet the hanger or decide the length of another hanger material.
2. Attach it on top of the logo between the pieces i.e. place the end of the round at the bottom, and thus the top will be one corner of the logo.
3. If you are going to sew the hanger, do it now to the top corner of both pieces.
   If you are going to crochet the hanger, then only mark the place for it for now.
4. Start crocheting around from the **bottom** of the pieces.
   This way, the last closing stitches after filling will be at the bottom and more hidden than if they were on the top corner.
5. When reaching the top corner and the hanger, either crochet over it and attach it on the go, or try to skip it cleanly if it was sewn earlier.
